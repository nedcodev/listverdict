Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026

What we collect

When you start using Listverdict, we ask for your email address. We also record whether you ticked the box to receive tips and updates.

That's all we collect. The listings, tags, view counts and verdicts you enter stay in your own browser on your own device. They are never sent to us.

Why we collect your email

  • To give you access to the free tools.
  • If you ticked the box, to send you tips and Listverdict updates.

If you didn't tick the box, we won't send you marketing emails.

Who handles it

Email addresses are stored with Kit, the email service we use to manage our list. We don't sell or share your email with anyone else.

Your choices

  • Every email we send has an unsubscribe link.
  • To have your email deleted, reply to any email from us or message our Instagram account, and we'll remove it.
  • To delete your saved tests, clear this site's data in your browser, or delete them one by one under My tests.

Not affiliated with Etsy

Listverdict is an independent tool. It doesn't connect to your Etsy account and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Etsy, Inc.
