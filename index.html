      How the verdict stays fair

      Views rise and fall for reasons that have nothing to do with your change, like holidays, Etsy sales events or a slow week. So Listverdict also looks at the rest of your shop over the same days, and only credits your change with the difference.

      Undo

      Too close to call
      Anything in between. Give it more time, or keep whichever you prefer.
      Not enough data
      Fewer than 50 views before the change. Small numbers swing too much to judge.

      It's a fairer estimate, not a lab test. Things outside your shop, like a competitor's sale, can still move your views.
